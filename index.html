Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher working at Paderborn University. My research focuses on Reinforcement Learning, Diffusion Models for curriculum generation, Sample-Efficient Robotic Manipulation, and Safe Generative AI & Control Barrier Functions for autonomous systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in the Chair of Robotics, AI and Real-time Systems under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Alois Knoll in collaboration with the Human Brain Project.

Prior to TUM, I earned my M.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from RWTH Aachen University (Grade: 1.0). I completed dual Bachelor's degrees in Mechatronics Engineering from Kocaeli University, where I graduated as Valedictorian (ranked 1st across the entire Faculty of Engineering), and Bochum University of Applied Sciences.
